How Large Language Models Like ChatGPT Actually Work

Large language models can write, summarize, translate, explain concepts and generate computer code. But how does a machine actually produce language that looks so intelligent?

What is a large language model?

A large language model, or LLM, is an artificial intelligence system trained to process and generate human language. It learns statistical patterns from very large collections of text and uses those patterns to predict what text should come next.

The important point is that an LLM does not simply store a giant database of answers. During training, it adjusts a very large number of internal parameters so that it becomes better at predicting language.

Everything starts with tokens

Language models generally do not process text exactly as humans see it. Text is divided into smaller pieces called tokens.

A token can represent a complete word, part of a word, punctuation, or another piece of text. The exact way text is divided depends on the model's tokenizer.

Simple example:

A sentence such as "Artificial intelligence is changing technology" can be broken into a sequence of smaller tokens before being processed by the model.

Training the model

During training, the model is exposed to enormous amounts of information and repeatedly attempts to predict missing or upcoming tokens.

If the prediction is incorrect, the training process adjusts the model's parameters. Repeating this process on huge numbers of examples allows the model to learn increasingly complex patterns.

What are neural networks?

Modern language models are based on artificial neural networks. These networks contain many layers of mathematical operations that transform information as it moves through the system.

The model's parameters contain numerical values that are adjusted during training. Together, these parameters allow the network to represent complex relationships between words, concepts and patterns in language.

The Transformer architecture

Most modern large language models are based on the Transformer architecture. Transformers became extremely important because they can process relationships between different parts of a sequence efficiently.

One of their most important mechanisms is called attention.

What does attention do?

Attention allows the model to determine which other parts of the input are particularly relevant when processing a particular token.

For example, in a long sentence, a word near the end may depend on information introduced much earlier. Attention helps the model connect these pieces of information.

How does an LLM generate an answer?

When you send a prompt to an LLM, the text is first converted into tokens. Those tokens are processed by the neural network.

The model then calculates probabilities for possible next tokens. A token is selected according to the model's generation process, added to the sequence and processed again.

This happens repeatedly until the model produces the response.

The key idea:

An LLM generates text step by step. It repeatedly predicts what token should come next based on the context available to it.

Why does it appear to understand?

The patterns learned during training can be extremely sophisticated. The model can learn relationships between words, concepts, syntax, facts and different styles of writing.

This can produce behavior that looks like deep understanding. However, the internal mechanism is still based on mathematical operations performed by a neural network.

Why can LLMs make mistakes?

A language model's primary mechanism is generating likely text. That does not guarantee that every generated statement is factually correct.

A model can therefore produce an answer that sounds convincing while containing incorrect information. These errors are commonly referred to as AI hallucinations.

Why training requires enormous computing power

Training a large model involves processing enormous quantities of data through very large neural networks.

Specialized processors such as GPUs and other accelerators are used to perform the massive number of mathematical operations required.

Large-scale training can therefore require substantial computing resources, electricity, storage and networking infrastructure.

Training is not the only stage

After initial training, advanced AI systems can go through additional processes designed to make their behavior more useful, safer and better aligned with the desired interaction style.

These stages can involve additional datasets, human feedback, evaluation and other techniques.

The bigger picture

Large language models combine several major areas of computing: machine learning, neural networks, natural language processing, mathematics, high-performance computing and massive-scale data processing.

Their impressive capabilities come from the interaction of these components rather than from one simple piece of technology.

Key takeaway:

An LLM learns statistical patterns from large amounts of data using neural networks. When you give it a prompt, it processes the tokens and repeatedly predicts the next token to construct a response.
